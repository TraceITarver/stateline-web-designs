Our story

Family recipes, open fire.

Bayou Ember Kitchen started with a cast-iron pot and a grandmother's gumbo recipe. Today we cook the same way: low, slow, and with plenty of love — for neighbors, families, and anyone hungry for a taste of Louisiana.
